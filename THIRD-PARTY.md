# Third-party components

The proprietary launcher license applies only to original WehniX software. Wine, FEX, DXMT, LLVM, Neptune and other components retain their own licenses and source rights.

This free beta retains its bundled Game Porting Toolkit, Wine and Linux runtimes. Apple component use and redistribution remain subject to the supplied Apple license, including its noncommercial restrictions. Component notices and provenance are inside the runtime packages.

Source materials and their exact upstream revisions are attached to the beta release where available. The source-material index describes what each archive contains and identifies remaining provenance gaps; it does not claim that an upstream archive alone proves a complete corresponding-source match for every bundled dependency.

No source-availability flag or redistribution approval has been fabricated. Contact the project through the repository issues for component/source questions, without posting private account data.
